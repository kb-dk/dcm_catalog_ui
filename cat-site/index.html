Catalogues of Selected Composers' Works

Thematic-bibliographic catalogues of the works of selected composers in Denmark


  • CNW: Catalogue of Carl Nielsen's Works
  • NWGW: Niels W. Gade. Thematic-Bibliographic Catalogue of His Works
  • SchW: Johann Adolph Scheibe. A Catalogue of His Works
  • HartW: J.P.E. Hartmann. Thematic-Bibliographic Catalogue of His Works


The MEI XML datasets behind these catalogues are available at the Royal Danish Library's repository for research data: https://loar.kb.dk/handle/1902/49096
